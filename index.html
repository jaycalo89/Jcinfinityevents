Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

These Terms & Conditions apply to rental and service bookings with JC Infinity Events LLC. Specific packages, services, equipment, inclusions, pricing, taxes, and approved add-ons are identified in the Client's invoice or accepted estimate and Rental & Service Agreement.

1. Reservation and Payment

A 50% non-refundable retainer of the total invoice amount is required to reserve the Client's event date.

The remaining balance is due seven (7) days before the event date.

The event date is not officially reserved until the required retainer has been received and the Rental & Service Agreement has been signed.

JC Infinity Events LLC may cancel delivery or setup if the remaining balance is not paid by the due date.

2. Refundable Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit of $150.00 is due with the final balance, no later than seven (7) days before the event.

The security deposit is separate from the rental price and is held to cover loss or damage to JC Infinity Events LLC equipment during the rental period.

The security deposit will be returned within five (5) business days after pickup and inspection of the equipment, provided the equipment is returned in substantially the same condition as delivered, excluding normal wear and tear.

JC Infinity Events LLC may deduct reasonable and documented repair or replacement costs for loss or damage caused by the Client or the Client's guests. Any remaining balance of the security deposit will be refunded to the Client.

3. Cancellation Policy

The 50% booking retainer is non-refundable once the event date has been reserved.

If the Client cancels more than fourteen (14) days before the event, any amount paid above the non-refundable retainer will be refunded.

If the Client cancels fourteen (14) days or less before the event, all payments made toward the rental or service are non-refundable.

Any refundable security deposit that has already been paid will be returned, provided no rental equipment has been delivered or placed at risk.

If the Client has paid the full rental or service balance and cancels more than fourteen (14) days before the event, JC Infinity Events LLC will retain the 50% retainer and refund the remaining rental or service amount paid.

4. Rescheduling

Requests to reschedule are subject to availability.

If JC Infinity Events LLC is available on the new requested date, the Company may transfer the Client's payments to the new date.

If the new date is unavailable, the cancellation terms above will apply.

5. Rental Period

Unless otherwise stated in the Client's invoice or accepted estimate, the selected guestbook package includes up to six (6) hours of guestbook availability.

The six-hour period begins at the agreed guestbook start time, not when JC Infinity Events LLC arrives for setup.

Additional time must be approved in advance and may require an additional fee.

All rental equipment remains the property of JC Infinity Events LLC.
